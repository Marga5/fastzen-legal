Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

FastZen ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the FastZen mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: email address and display name, collected when you create an account via email/password or Google Sign-In.
  • Health and fitness data: fasting sessions (start time, end time, duration, plan type) and body weight entries that you manually log in the app.
  • Usage data: app interactions and performance data collected anonymously via Firebase Analytics.
  • Purchase information: subscription status managed via Google Play. We do not store your payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise the fasting tracker experience.
  • To sync your data securely across devices.
  • To manage your Premium subscription status.
  • To send fasting reminders via push notifications (only if you grant permission).
  • To improve the app through anonymous usage analytics.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore), hosted in the European Union. All data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

4. Third-Party Services

FastZen uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, database, and analytics — privacy policy
  • Google Sign-In: optional login method — privacy policy
  • Google AdMob: advertising shown to free users — privacy policy
  • Google Play Billing: in-app subscription management — privacy policy

5. Advertising

Free users may see advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show personalised ads. Premium subscribers do not see ads. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with the third-party services listed above, as necessary to operate the app.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data (fasting sessions, weight logs, and account information) is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data (via Settings → Delete Account in the app, or by contacting us).
  • Withdraw consent to data processing at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

FastZen is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
