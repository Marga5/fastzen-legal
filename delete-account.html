Delete Your Account

You can delete your FastZen account and all associated data directly from within the app. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the FastZen app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. All your data will be erased and cannot be recovered.

The following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your account (email address and display name)
  • All fasting sessions and history
  • All weight log entries
  • Your settings and preferences
  • Your streak and statistics

Data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days of the deletion request.

Note: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Premium subscription. Please cancel your subscription via the Google Play Store → Subscriptions before or after deleting your account.

If you are unable to access the app or need assistance, you can request account deletion by contacting us:
